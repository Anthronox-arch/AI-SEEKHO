## Part 1: Agentic IDE Research

### Cursor

**What is it and who built it:** Cursor is an AI-powered IDE, built by Anysphere, that is built upon the open source VS Code but adds many AI features. Unlike VS Code, it can understand a whole project instead of a single file.

**Main Features:**
- AI chat inside the IDE.
- For completing larger tasks, agent mode is available.
- Editing many files at the same time.
- Understands the whole codebase/project.
- Code generation and debugging.

**Codebase Integration:**
- Context is made better by indexing the whole project.
- Supports MCP (Model Context Protocol).
- Compatible with many extensions, including those used in VS Code.
- Git integration.

**Pricing:**
- Free plan available but with limited usage.
- Pro plan for higher limits and more powerful models.

**Key Difference:**

Cursor is one of the best AI-first IDE with extraordinary understanding of large projects/codebases.

**My Opinion:**
- Strengths:
  - Powerful.
  - Easy to use and great UI.
  - Very fast
- Limitations:
  - Limited on free plan.
  - AI suggestions still need human review.

**Best Use Case:**

Building medium to large projects such as portfolio projects.

## Part 2: Prompt Engineering

### Scenario 1: The Budget Trip

You are an expert travel planner that helps in low-budget travel planning within Pakistan. I have PKR 15,000 total. I want to travel with two friends. We have 3 free days and cannot spend more than this budget. Within this amount of money, we want transportation, accommodation, food and sightseeing. Suggest the best destination in Pakistan that fits the budget. Include transportation options, estimated costs, hotel recommendations, local food, tourist attractions, and a detailed schedule for every day. Give the result in a clean manner with clear headings, title, and descriptions.

### Scenario 2: The Difficult Message

You are a professional communication expert. My landlord has ignored my requests to repair a broken water heater for two weeks. I have already asked twice verbally. I need to remain respectful because later, I want to renew my lease while taking a firm stand to drive results. Write a firm but polite WhatsApp message requesting that the heater be repaired before winter. The message should explain the problem without sounding rude. Keep the message under 200 words with a respectful and professional tone.

### Scenario 3: The Impossible Schedule

You are a productivity and time-management expert/coach. I am a 6th-semester student taking five courses, working 15 hours per week, finishing a side project due in three weeks, and wanting to go to the gym four times each week. I don't commit because my schedules aren't good. Create a weekly timetable that balances study, work, gym, project work, meals, rest, and sleep. Consider energy levels instead of simply filling every available hour. Present the schedule as a weekly table.

### Scenario 4: The Broken Student Meal Plan

You are a nutritionist who is an expert in affordable student meal planning. I have PKR 8,000 per month for food. I cook in a hostel kitchen with only a stove and a small refrigerator. I want healthier meals that improve my energy while staying within budget. Create a 7-day meal plan with breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks. Include easy recipes and a grocery shopping list. Show the grocery list with estimated prices and explain how the total stays within budget.

### Scenario 5: Scholarship Interview

You are an experienced scholarship interviewer. I have a scholarship interview in five days. I become nervous during interviews, freeze under pressure, and tend to give long, unfocused answers. The panel usually asks about weaknesses, future goals, and why I deserve the scholarship. Conduct a realistic mock interview by asking one question at a time. After each answer, provide feedback on the simplicity of my answers, confidence, and structure of the answer, then suggest improvements before moving to the next question. At the end, summarize my strengths, weaknesses, and practical tips to improve before the interview.
